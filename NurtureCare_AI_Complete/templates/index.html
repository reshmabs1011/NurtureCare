PROJECT INFORMATION

About / Guidelines

Designed for accessible, understandable maternal-health support.

ⓘ
✚

Project vision

AI-Powered Maternal Health Risk Screening and Emergency Referral System. NurtureCare AI aims to help pregnant women and community health workers record selected health information, recognize possible warning signs, and find referral support—especially in rural and underserved areas.

⌁

Rural accessibility first

No login barrier, readable controls, responsive layout, regional-language selector placeholder, and local draft queue are included in this prototype. Translation strings and full offline CDN replacement still need further implementation.

⚕

Medical safety

This is a hackathon prototype, not a diagnosis, medical device, or replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. Rules are illustrative and have not been clinically validated. A reassuring result cannot rule out a complication.

▣

Privacy and data

Use synthetic/demo data only. Browser local storage is not encrypted. Before real deployment, implement informed consent, secure access, encryption, retention controls, clinical review, and applicable privacy requirements.

!

URGENT MEDICAL GUIDANCE

Seek urgent medical care now

Severe warning symptoms need immediate professional assessment. Do not wait for the website, a model result, an internet connection, or a callback.

Call emergency services
Verify local emergency contacts before deploying this prototype outside India.
